      The numbers come from Google Trends. They measure what people search for, not what they buy, which makes them an early signal of demand rather than a sales figure.

      Each market is scaled so its most searched product scores 100, so markets cannot be compared on volume. Europe is the average of the market scales.

      Google compares five terms at a time. Products are chained through one reference product (the first one in the list), so small products are approximate.

      Changes compare the latest weeks with the weeks before and include seasonality: coats rise every autumn.
